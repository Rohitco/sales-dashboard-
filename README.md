📱 Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI
  An interactive Mobile Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze mobile sales performance, transactions, customer ratings, payment methods, brands, models, and   daily/monthly trends.

📊 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of mobile sales performance with interactive filters and visualizations.

Key KPIs
Total Sales: 308M
Total Quantity Sold: 8K
Total Transactions: 2K
Average Transaction Value: 41K

KPI values shown above are based on the dashboard snapshot and may change when filters are applied.

🔎 Dashboard Features
1. Sales Performance
Total sales by mobile model
Total sales by day
Monthly units sold trend
Brand-wise sales comparison

2. Customer Analysis
Customer rating distribution from 1 to 5
Count of customer ratings
Identification of rating patterns and customer satisfaction trends

3. Transaction Analysis
Transaction distribution by payment method
Payment methods include:

Credit Card
UPI
Debit Card
Cash

4. Geographic Analysis
Interactive map showing sales/unit activity across major Indian cities.

5. Interactive Filters
Users can dynamically filter the dashboard by:
Mobile Model
Payment Method
Brand
Day
Month

📈 Key Insights

From the dashboard snapshot:
Apple has the highest total sales among the displayed brands.
The dashboard records approximately 7,622 units in total quantity.
The highest customer-rating count is for 5-star ratings.
Credit Card, UPI, Debit Card, and Cash contribute relatively balanced transaction volumes.
Monthly unit sales fluctuate throughout the year, with noticeable peaks in February, June, August, September, and November.
Mobile models such as iPhone SE, Galaxy S21, and OnePlus Nord are among the highlighted models by sales.

Explore the visualizations to identify sales and customer trends.
